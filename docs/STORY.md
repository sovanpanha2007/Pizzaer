# Pizzaer — Story & Dialogue

## 1. Story Introduction

Grandpa Lim's pizza shop is finally open!  
But on the first day, the delivery boy suddenly quits.  
You are the only helper left to deliver the pizzas.  
Complete five deliveries and help save the shop's big day!

## 2. Main Character

**Chef's Name:** Chef Lim

Chef Lim is the owner and head chef of Grandpa Lim's Pizza Shop. He prepares pizzas and gives delivery orders to the player.

**Chef's First Dialogue:**

"Welcome! I'm Chef Lim. We have five pizzas to deliver today. Let's save the shop!"
